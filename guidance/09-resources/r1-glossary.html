Terms and abbreviations used in this playbook, in alphabetical order.

A to B

Glossary terms A to B and what they mean
Term Meaning
Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard, ATRS How a public body publishes what an algorithmic tool does, what it is for, what data it was built on, how it affects decisions, who is accountable for it and what oversight it has.

See algorithmic transparency and AI for when it is mandatory and what it asks for
Analysis Senior Responsible Officer The named person accountable for Business Critical analysis or a model across its lifecycle. They confirm it is suitable for its intended use before it is used.

NHSBSA’s documents also call this role the Senior Responsible Owner (SRO), the Analysis Responsible Officer, the Model Senior Responsible Officer or the approver. See roles and responsibilities
Analyst The person who plans, does and documents the analysis
Analytical Assurance Decision Tool NHSBSA’s scoring tool: six impact dimensions against six likelihood dimensions, read on a four by four matrix.

See deciding your assurance level for how to use it. The workbook is held on the NHSBSA SharePoint, which needs an NHSBSA account with access to the Analytical Quality and Assurance site
Analytical lifecycle The five phases a piece of analysis runs through: engagement and scoping; design; analysis; delivery, communication and sign-off; and review.

The five phases of the analytical lifecycle
Analytical model Analysis built to be run more than once, typically over time. One of eight types.

The eight types of analytical model
Analytical output Anything that results from collecting, manipulating and interpreting data to answer a specific question. Examples include a dataset for a Freedom of Information request, an official statistics report, a dashboard and a forecasting model.

Everything in this playbook applies to all of them. Check the playbook applies
Analytical Quality and Assurance function The team that holds all analytical documentation centrally, and monitors Business Critical assurance activity. The Data Science, Statistics and AI team holds the Business Critical register.

Business Critical analysis, step 4
Analytical Quality and Assurance Lead The organisational role that confirms assurance levels and resolves level disputes. It is also the route when the commissioner and the analyst cannot resolve a Business Critical decision or an ethical concern.

The governance and management framework records who holds it
AQuA Book The government’s guidance on analytical quality assurance, which applies to all NHSBSA analysis alongside GovS 010.

Which standards apply to you
Artificial intelligence, AI Software that performs tasks which would otherwise need human judgement, including machine learning models.

Analysis that builds, uses or evaluates an AI system has extra obligations. Algorithmic transparency and AI
Assurance level The proportionate amount of quality assurance a piece of analysis needs.

Level 1 Standard, Level 2 Enhanced, Level 3 Comprehensive or Level 4 BCA, read from the Analytical Assurance Decision Tool. The level is complete at design, or at scoping where an impact dimension scored 4.

Older NHSBSA documents may say “Tier” where the playbook says “Level”, and the two map one for one: “Tier 3” is Level 3 Comprehensive. Each level includes the checks below it
Assurance plan What will be checked, by whom and when, written at design once the level is known, in the quality assurance plan template. Required from Level 2.

Step 4, what your level requires
BCA Business Critical analysis. The abbreviation appears in the name of the fourth assurance level, Level 4 BCA
Business Critical Analysis that scores 4, very high, on any one of the six impact dimensions of the Analytical Assurance Decision Tool.

Registered, and assured at Level 4 BCA. Settled at scoping. NHSBSA’s definition is on Business Critical analysis
Business Critical register Where Business Critical work is recorded.

The entry is made at scoping. Each entry names an Analysis Senior Responsible Officer. Business Critical analysis, step 4

C to F

Glossary terms C to F and what they mean
Term Meaning
Code of Practice for Statistics The standard official statistics follow, in addition to GovS 010 and the AQuA Book. Its three pillars are trustworthiness, quality and value.

What each standard asks for
Commissioner The person requesting the analysis, who owns the decision it supports.

A commissioner may be an internal team, an external partner, or a sponsor such as the Department of Health and Social Care or NHS England. Commissioners can also be non-analysts or third-party suppliers. Who counts as a commissioner
Correction A change made to a published figure because something was wrong.

Graded by what the change does to the conclusions rather than by how big the number is. Correcting a published figure
Critical friend, analytical assurer Two names for one role: someone not delivering the analysis who gives independent advice and confirms it is fit for purpose.

Critical friend is the name NHSBSA’s playbooks use; analytical assurer is the standards’ term, at GovS 010 4.4.6 and AQuA Book 5.1. See roles and responsibilities
Data and AI Ethics Framework The seven principles for using data and AI responsibly, with a self-assessment tool.

Data ethics and personal data
Data protection impact assessment, DPIA The corporate assessment of what using personal data risks, and how those risks are managed.

It is an information governance process rather than an analytical one. Data ethics and personal data says where to go for it
Decision log A record of the choices made during the analysis, and of anything that departed from the plan, written as they happen. Required from Level 2
Decommissioning Stopping production of an output that is no longer useful. A finding of review rather than a failure.

Phase 5, review
Direct uncertainty Uncertainty about an estimate that can be measured or quantified
DSIT The Department for Science, Innovation and Technology
Freedom of Information, FOI A request made under the Freedom of Information Act.

NHSBSA’s worked examples put an FOI response at impact 3, with its visibility scored under public visibility and reach rather than added on top. The assurance level follows from that impact and your likelihood

G to L

Glossary terms G to L and what they mean
Term Meaning
Government Data Quality Framework The government guidance on managing and describing data quality. It applies where data quality is a material concern for your work.

Which standards apply to you
Government Functional Standard, GovS The standards that set mandatory expectations across government functions. GovS 010 Analysis is the one this playbook rests on.

Others referred to here are GovS 005 Digital, GovS 008 Commercial and GovS 011 Communication
GRADE scale An ordered scale for grading how much confidence to place in a body of evidence. One way of summarising indirect uncertainty, which is inferred rather than measured directly
Green, Magenta and Orange Books Government guidance on appraisal, evaluation and risk management. Each applies where your work is that kind of work.

Which standards apply to you
Impact dimension One of the six measures of impact scored on the Analytical Assurance Decision Tool.

Each is scored Level 1 to Level 4
Indirect uncertainty Uncertainty about the underlying quality of knowledge surrounding an estimate
Lead Assurer At Level 4 BCA, the assurer from outside the delivering team who signs the quality assurance sign-off before the Analysis Senior Responsible Officer approves the work
Lead Official for Statistics NHSBSA’s title for the organisational role that is the route for whether an output is fit to publish, and for advice on official statistics.

The Code of Practice for Statistics calls the same role the Head of Profession for Statistics.

The governance and management framework records who holds it
Likelihood dimension One of the six measures of likelihood scored on the Analytical Assurance Decision Tool.

Each is scored Level 1 to Level 4, at design

M to R

Glossary terms M to R and what they mean
Term Meaning
NHSBSA The NHS Business Services Authority
Office for Statistics Regulation, OSR The regulator that monitors and reports on the UK statistical system, and assesses compliance with the Code of Practice for Statistics.

A regulatory concern about an NHSBSA output is scored under reputation in score the impact
Official statistics Statistics produced under the Statistics and Registration Service Act, governed by the Code of Practice for Statistics
Peer review Review of the work by somebody who did not produce it. What counts as independent enough depends on the level.

The critical friend
Phase One of the five parts of the analytical lifecycle, Phase 1 to Phase 5.

Phase 1, engagement and scoping is the first of them
Project record The one place everything about a piece of analysis is recorded. Nine sections, of which your level decides how many you complete.

What to record
Quality assurance, QA Everything done to make analysis trustworthy and to show that it is.

Some Business Critical documents carry it in their own names, such as the QA appraisal in Business Critical analysis
Quality assurance appraisal The Business Critical workbook. Twelve worksheets completed at three points: basic information, the model review, and the assurance review by the Lead Assurer.

Document 6 and document 10 of the stack in Business Critical analysis
Quality assurance log The record of checks carried out, when, by whom, and what was found. Kept under QA evidence and records in the quality assurance plan
Quality assurance statement The published or recorded account of what was done to make analysis trustworthy, and what its limits are
Reproducible Analytical Pipeline, RAP An approach to building an analysis as code, so that it can be re-run and reproduced, with automation where that is proportionate.

For the NHS England guidance, see Links to standards and guides
Revalidation Confirming that a designation is still right.

Level 3 and Level 4 work is reviewed annually, and before any reuse for a new purpose. A new purpose is the change most likely to invalidate the assumptions the original was built on
Revision An expected change to published statistics: provisional figures finalised, a change of methodology, or the arrival of further data.

A revision is not an error, and a correction is. Correcting a published figure
RIGOUR Repeatable, Independent, Grounded in reality, Objective, Uncertainty-managed, Robust

S to Z

Glossary terms S to Z and what they mean
Term Meaning
Scope The document that sets out the decisions the analysis supports, its inputs and outputs, its boundaries and limitations, the resources it needs, and its principal risks. Written before the Specification.

Document 2 of the stack in Business Critical analysis
Senior officer accountable for analysis The organisational role GovS 010 requires NHSBSA to appoint, accountable for analysis as an activity rather than for approving any one output.

The governance and management framework records who holds it
Sensitivity testing Varying a key assumption to see whether the conclusion moves. Planned at design rather than added afterwards
Sign-off Confirmation that the analysis has been assured to the standard its assurance level asks for. Who signs depends on your level.

The commissioner then signs the output off for release, at every level, in Phase 4. Who signs at each level
Single point of contact The role the Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard asks of an organisation in its mandatory scope. It coordinates with the standard’s own team on identifying the tools in scope, drafting the records and publishing them.

See algorithmic transparency and AI
Specification The document agreed with your commissioner that sets out the question, the scope and the assurance required.

Written at scoping with the impact score, and returned to at design once the level is complete. Business Critical work carries the finished level from scoping
SRO Senior Responsible Owner. Another name NHSBSA uses for the role this playbook calls the Analysis Senior Responsible Officer. See roles and responsibilities
Statistical disclosure control, SDC Protecting individuals from being identified in published figures. NHSBSA has its own protocol.

NHSBSA templates and documents
Step A numbered action within a single page, such as step 4 of Business Critical analysis. The exception is the four steps of the Analytical Assurance Decision Tool, which are four pages numbered 1 to 4
Triangulation Reproducing a result independently from source, to check it. First required at Level 2
UK General Data Protection Regulation, UK GDPR The data protection legislation that applies in the UK. Article 22 prohibits decisions based solely on automated processing where they have legal or similarly significant consequences for a person.

See algorithmic transparency and AI
Validation Confirming the right type of analysis was used
Verification Confirming the analysis was conducted properly
